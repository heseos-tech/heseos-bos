# Heseos Installer — Google Play listing

Package name: `com.heseos.installer`

**App name** (max 30): `Heseos Installer`

**Short description** (max 80):
> Claim smart-home installation jobs in your city and track your earnings.

**Full description**:
> Heseos Installer is the job app for Heseos Technologies' installation partners.
>
> With the app you can:
> • See installation jobs open in your city — with the full list of products to be installed and the fee you will earn for each job
> • Claim a job first-come-first-served; once it is yours, the customer's contact details and address unlock
> • Call, WhatsApp or open the customer's location in maps in one tap
> • Mark a job complete and track pending and paid earnings
> • Refer new customers to Heseos using your own QR code and referral link, and follow each lead's progress
> • Manage your profile and the bank details used for payouts
>
> Installation fees are settled by Heseos outside the app (bank transfer/UPI) — the app is for tracking and management, not for moving money.
>
> This app requires a Heseos Installation Partner account created by the Heseos team. To become an installation partner, contact Heseos Technologies.
